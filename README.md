## What is this
This is a little project I've been working on. The goal is to simulate redstone, and to eventually end up bruteforcing some circuits to help redstoners maker more compact machines.

## Controls

- WASD or middle click drag to move around
- Scroll wheel to zoom in and out
- Middle click to copy a block

When nothing in hand:
- Right click and drag to delete
- Left click to press buttons / levers

When holding a block:
- Space to change its state (ex: 1t repeater to 2t repeater)
- Ctrl + Scroll wheel to change power of the block
- Left click and drag to place
- Right click to unselect
